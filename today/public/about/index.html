InFlite brings together the people you talk to, the plans you make and the information you check every day.

Meet the apps.

Start with an overview in Today, then go straight to the app you need.

  • Today

    See your forecast, plans and the things that need your attention in a single view.

  • Mail

    Read, write and follow up on email. Find the people you write to through your contacts.

  • Calendar

    Keep appointments and reminders together, and see where they fit in your day.

  • Notes

    Capture a thought, write a longer note or work through a checklist.

  • People

    Keep contact details and follow-ups together so the next conversation is easier to start.

  • Files

    Keep documents and photos in your Drive and find them again when you need them.

  • Chats

    Send messages to friends and follow the journey of the pigeon carrying your words.

  • Weather

    Check the forecast, rainfall and weather alerts for places you care about.

  • Fitness

    Plan training, review activities and see how your progress changes over time.

  • Markets

    Follow prices, compare charts and keep a watchlist of the markets you follow.
